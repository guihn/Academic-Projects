# Number Base Conversions

## Description

The activity asks for the same numeric quantities to be represented in different bases. Each conversion requires attention to positional values and the digits allowed in the destination system. The ten items include conversions to and from decimal as well as direct binary–hexadecimal conversions.

## Statement

Perform the following conversions:

| Item | Number | Original&nbsp;base | Destination&nbsp;base |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | `12345` | 10 | 16 |
| 02 | `ABCDEF` | 16 | 10 |
| 03 | `12345` | 10 | 2 |
| 04 | `10101010101` | 2 | 10 |
| 05 | `A000001` | 16 | 2 |
| 06 | `F10101` | 16 | 2 |
| 07 | `101010101010101` | 2 | 16 |
| 08 | `100000011111` | 2 | 16 |
| 09 | `12345` | 8 | 10 |
| 10 | `12345` | 10 | 8 |
